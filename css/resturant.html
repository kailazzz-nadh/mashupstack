Los Pollos Hermanos

Hermanos is featured as a front organization for Gus Fring's methamphetamine manufacturing and distribution, but is also highly regarded by the general public of the Southwest as a regional chain on par with KFC. The set used for the restaurant's Albuquerque location in the show was at a series of Twisters locations in South Valley, New Mexico. As a result of this, Twisters has seen an increase in business attributed to being associated with Breaking Bad. Due to the show's popularity, Los Pollos Hermanos has appeared on numerous occasions as a real-life pop-up restaurant.
