Methylamine

Methylamine (CH₃NH₂) is the simplest primary amine, consisting of a methyl group attached to an amino group. It is a colorless gas with a strong ammonia-like odor and is highly soluble in water. Methylamine is widely used in the manufacture of pharmaceuticals, pesticides, dyes, and various industrial chemicals.Read more

Acetamide

Acetamide (CH₃CONH₂) is the simplest amide derived from acetic acid. It is a white, crystalline solid that is odorless and highly soluble in water. Acetamide is used as a solvent, plasticizer, and intermediate in the manufacture of pharmaceuticals, pesticides, and other organic compounds.Read more
